Medicine Overdose Prevention

Learn how to prevent medication errors and maintain a healthy lifestyle

Common Causes of Drug Overdose

Understanding the common causes can help prevent medication errors

Drug overdoses can happen for various reasons, including:

  • Taking incorrect dosages of medication
  • Mixing medications without medical advice
  • Using expired medications
  • Not following prescription instructions
  • Self-medicating without proper knowledge
  • Accidental ingestion by children
  • Using medications prescribed for someone else

Maintaining a Healthy Body

Tips for keeping your body healthy and preventing health issues

Healthy Eating

Eat a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins.

Regular Exercise

Aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate exercise most days of the week.

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water throughout the day to keep your body functioning properly.

Adequate Sleep

Get 7-8 hours of quality sleep each night to allow your body to repair and recharge.

Avoid Harmful Substances

Limit alcohol consumption and avoid tobacco and illicit drugs.

Mental Health

Practice stress management techniques and seek help when needed.

Healthy Food Choices

Making better food choices for a healthier lifestyle

Foods to Include:

  • Fresh fruits and vegetables
  • Whole grains (oats, brown rice, quinoa)
  • Lean proteins (chicken, fish, beans)
  • Healthy fats (avocado, nuts, olive oil)
  • Low-fat dairy products

Foods to Limit:

  • Processed foods
  • Sugary drinks and snacks
  • High-sodium foods
  • Saturated and trans fats
  • Refined carbohydrates

Additional Health Tips

More ways to maintain your health and prevent medication issues

Get Enough Sleep

Aim for 7 to 8 hours of quality sleep each night. Proper sleep helps your body repair itself and improves cognitive function.

Stop Smoking

If you smoke or use other tobacco products, seek help to quit. You don't have to do it alone - many resources are available.

Limit Alcohol Intake

Drink in moderation: up to one drink per day for women and two for men. Excessive alcohol can lead to health problems.

What counts as a drink?

  • 12 ounces of beer
  • 5 ounces of wine
  • 1.5 ounces of liquor

Remember that these are general guidelines. Some people should avoid alcohol completely, including those who are pregnant, taking certain medications, or have specific health conditions.
